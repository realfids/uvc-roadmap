## DARPA Active Portfolio — Trauma and Hemorrhagic Shock

### 1. DARPA Biostasis Program ⭐ Most Relevant Conceptual Adjacent Program
- **[DARPA.mil/biostasis](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/biostasis)**
- **Goal:** Extend the "golden hour" by slowing cellular metabolism using chemical biology — drug-induced reversible suspended animation ("slow life to save life") — **without cold-chain cooling**
- **Approach:** Polymer chemistry, protein engineering, deep cell activity monitoring; repurposing FDA-approved drugs
- **Funding:** Up to **$23 million over 5 years** to Wyss Institute at Harvard (partnership since 2018)
- **Key 2024 finding:** Donepezil (FDA-approved Alzheimer's drug) induces torpor-like state in tadpoles (ACS Nano, August 21, 2024); SNC80 compound shows suspended animation effects in beating pig heart and human organ chips
- **Stage:** Animal model / preclinical — **multi-year timeline before human use**
- **Sources:** [DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/biostasis), [Military Times (2018)](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2018/03/26/darpa-wants-to-slow-life-to-save-life-with-program-that-extends-the-golden-hour/), [ClearanceJobs (2024)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2024/09/03/darpa-and-harvard-collaborate-to-explore-suspended-animation-using-alzheimers-drug/), [Wyss/EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1070448)

**Comparison to INTERCEPT:** Biostasis aims to slow metabolism broadly; A2A agonist aims to suppress IRI-specific inflammatory cascades at reperfusion. Different mechanisms, potentially complementary. Biostasis remains preclinical; INTERCEPT (if in clinical development) would not be competing for the same field position.

### 2. DARPA FSHARP — Field-Deployable Blood Substitute
- **[DARPA.mil/FSHARP](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/fieldable-solutions-for-hemorrhage-with-bio-artificial-resuscitation-products)**
- **Goal:** Shelf-stable, field-deployable, universal whole blood substitute; 6 months stability without cold chain
- **Funding:** **$46.4 million, 4-year program**; led by University of Maryland School of Medicine
- **Key partners:** Case Western Reserve, KaloCyte (ErythroMer nanoparticle RBC surrogate), Haima Therapeutics (SynthoPlate platelet substitute), Latham Biopharm Group, Ohio State, Charles River Labs
- **Stage:** Active development — products in preclinical/early clinical testing
- **Sources:** [DARPA News 2023](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2023/deployable-whole-blood-equivalent), [UMSOM 2023](https://www.medschool.umaryland.edu/news/2023/artificial-blood-product-one-step-closer-to-reality-with-46-million-in-federal-funding.html)

**Comparison to INTERCEPT:** FSHARP targets volume replacement and oxygen-carrying capacity — the hemorrhagic physiology problem. A2A agonist targets the IRI-mediated inflammatory cascade at reperfusion — complementary, not competing.

---

### 3. DARPA MASH — Autonomous Hemorrhage Control Robotics
- **[DARPA.mil/MASH](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/medics-autonomously-stopping-hemorrhage)**
- **Goal:** Autonomous robotic systems that detect, localize, and stop non-compressible torso hemorrhage (NCTH) without a surgeon; sustain casualties for 48+ hours
- **Structure:** 3-year program, two phases: sensor integration → software/autonomy
- **NCTH causes >50% of potentially survivable combat deaths**
- **Sources:** [EverGlade](https://everglade.com/darpas-mash-program-autonomous-trauma-care-for-the-battlefield/), [DefenseMirror](https://www.defensemirror.com/news/40243/DARPA_Unveils_Robotic_Program_to_Halt_Deadly_Battlefield_Bleeding)

**Comparison to INTERCEPT:** MASH is mechanical/robotic hemorrhage control; orthogonal to pharmacological anti-IRI strategy.

---

### 4. DARPA GOLDEVAC — Multi-Function Intravascular Device
- **[DARPA Live Chain spotlight](https://www.darpa.mil/research/research-spotlights/live-chain)**
- **Goal:** Single intravascular catheter device that provides oxygenation, medication infusion, fluid/blood resuscitation, biological fluid sampling, and thrombosis management during medical evacuation
- **Launched:** March 2024
- Part of DARPA's broader **"Live Chain" portfolio** alongside MASH and FSHARP

### 5. DARPA Triage Challenge (DTC) + RITMO — AI/ML Triage Infrastructure

- DTC runs Fall 2023–Fall 2026; five tracks
- **RITMO:** Multi-site trauma patient database for AI triage signature detection; University of Maryland School of Medicine received **up to $7.3 million** (2023)

- **Sources:** [UMSOM 2023](https://www.medschool.umaryland.edu/news/2023/um-school-of-medicine-awarded-up-to-73m-from-darpa-to-drive-innovation-in-trauma-triage-technology-improve-mass-casualty-response-efforts/), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1011309)

### 6. Emergency Preservation and Resuscitation (EPR) — Historical DARPA/Army Program
- **[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Preservation_and_Resuscitation)** | **[UMSOM EPR doc](https://www.medschool.umaryland.edu/media/som/departments/anesthesiology/faculty--staff/certified-registered-nurse-anesthetists/docs/epr-trauma.pdf)**
- Peter Safar and Samuel Tisherman began EPR at Pittsburgh in 1980s
- Army Medical Research and Materiel Command invested **$17.5 million total** with Safar Center at Pitt
- **EPR-CAT clinical trial:** Penetrating trauma patients who arrest within 5 minutes of arrival; ice-cold saline intra-aortic flush to cool body to 10°C for 2+ hours of circulatory arrest during surgical repair
- Trials launched April 2014 (Pittsburgh), resumed ~2016 (Baltimore)
- Without EPR, target patient survival rate <7%

## Key Observations for SROI Speed-Up Estimate

1. **The field is active but weighted toward devices and blood products.** The pharmacological/novel drug side is comparatively less crowded. No active program was identified that mirrors INTERCEPT's A2A agonist + organ-on-chip + digital twin approach.

2. **Civilian prehospital translation is an explicitly acknowledged gap.** Multiple recent reviews (including *Current Trauma Reports*, 2025) note that military trauma advances are not being translated to civilian EMS systems at scale. INTERCEPT could occupy this translational gap.

3. **DARPA Biostasis is the closest adjacent program** to a "novel field drug for metabolic protection" concept. It remains at the animal model/preclinical stage with a multi-year timeline. The mechanism (broad metabolic slowing) is distinct from A2A agonism (targeted anti-inflammatory at reperfusion), and the programs are not directly competitive.

4. **CDMRP JWMRP explicitly funds "drugs that extend the physiologic resuscitation window"** — this is a direct potential funding vehicle for INTERCEPT's development program.

5. **Neither "TRACES" nor "PREVENT LOSS" appeared in any indexed public search results** — these may be internal working names, classified programs, or alternate names for programs listed above.

**SROI implication:** The selective A2A agonist prehospital pharmacological program occupies a **relatively uncrowded niche** within an otherwise active and well-funded field. The primary competing programs are in different mechanistic or delivery categories. Speed-up estimate should be **moderately positive** — the field is not neglected enough to indicate a major gap, but the specific niche of INTERCEPT is not crowded.
